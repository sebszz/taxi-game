# Night Shift Taxi 🚕

**[▶ Play in your browser](https://sebszz.github.io/taxi-game/)**: no download, works on PC, Mac, phones, tablets and Meta Quest (VR).

![Police chase](screenshots/police-chase.png)

Drive a yellow cab through a living city, from busy afternoons to neon-soaked nights. Pick up passengers, get them where they're going fast for bigger tips, and try to stay out of trouble. Or don't.

## What you can do
- **Pick up and drop off passengers**: green beams for pickups, orange for drop-offs. Faster trips earn bigger tips. Some fares wait up on the overpass.
- **Explore a whole city**: downtown skyscrapers with giant digital billboards, a Times Plaza crossroads, tunnels, two elevated expressways, roundabouts, a beach with palm trees, rough streets, restaurants and a building site with working tower cranes.
- **Day and night**: the sun sets and rises as you play, or pick your own time of day in Settings.
- **Get chased by the police**: speed past them, run red lights or hit someone, and you'll earn up to 3 wanted stars. Patrol cars, vans, motorbikes, roadblocks and a helicopter with a searchlight come after you, with chase music and police radio chatter. Lose them by getting out of sight, or hide in a garage.
- **Crash into things**: knock over lamp posts, traffic lights and trees, shove cars into spins, flatten tents, scatter cones and junk. Your cab dents, smokes and slows down as it takes damage.
- **Keep the cab running**: fill up at petrol stations and repair at one of 10 garages, each with its own prices.
- **Watch your step**: drive off the overpass or into the sea and it's game over, with a replay.
- **Play in VR** on a Meta Quest: sit in the driver's seat, steer with the thumbstick or grab the wheel with both hands, and check your mirrors.

## Controls
| Keyboard | Action |
|---|---|
| W A S D or arrow keys | Drive |
| Space | Handbrake |
| C | Change camera (behind, driver's seat, bonnet) |
| F | Fill up or repair |
| G | Garages and repair prices |
| O | Settings |
| P or Esc | Pause |

On phones and tablets, use the on-screen arrow buttons. In VR, the right trigger accelerates, the left trigger brakes, and **Y** opens the pause menu.

![Neon streets](screenshots/neon-streets.png)

## Settings worth knowing
- **Graphics**: Low, Medium or High.
- **Gameplay**: switch police chases, fuel or car damage on or off individually, for a relaxed drive.
- **Child-safe mode**: no blood, and anyone knocked over gets back up.

![Off the overpass](screenshots/off-the-overpass.png)

## About
The whole game is a single HTML file built with [three.js](https://threejs.org/). The city, cars, people and effects are all generated in code, and sound effects load from the `mp3` folder (with built-in sounds as a fallback when offline).
